# 📚🦘 QuizJumper — Educational Pixel-Art Jumper (working title)

A 2D pixel-art vertical **jumper** (Doodle Jump style) fused with a **Kahoot-style quiz** engine. Players jump upward while, at set points, the game freezes and everyone answers a question. Correct answers give a speed/jump **boost**; wrong answers **slow you down**. Play solo or with *N* players, with a live Formula-1-style leaderboard. Quizzes can be built by hand **or** auto-generated by AI from an uploaded PDF, then published for others to play.

## 1. Vision & Goals

- **Educational first.** The game is a delivery mechanism for learning content. Questions come from real study material (PDFs) or hand-made quizzes.
- **Fun loop.** Skill-based jumping + knowledge-based boosts means both reflexes *and* knowledge matter.
- **Social.** Solo or multiplayer, publishable quizzes, live competitive leaderboard.

## 2. Core Gameplay Loop

1. Player(s) join a game using a published quiz (20 questions by default).
2. The jumper auto-scrolls/climbs upward (Doodle Jump feel — jump on platforms, don't fall).
3. At each **question checkpoint**, the game **freezes** and shows a question to everyone.
4. **Answering does NOT immediately dismiss the question.** The question stays until:
   - the timer runs out (💡 **ASSUMPTION: 10 seconds**), **OR**
   - every player has answered.
5. **Correct answer → BOOST** (jump higher, move faster). **Wrong answer → SLOWDOWN.**
6. Answering **quickly + correctly → speed bonus points.**
7. After all 20 questions are answered, the game ends.
8. Final **placement** (by height/progress) awards points **F1-style** (1st gets the most, descending).

## 4. Question / Freeze Mechanics

- ✅ **DECIDED — trigger:** **Shared timer.** All players hit the same question at the same wall-clock moment (Q5 fires for everyone simultaneously), keeping the quiz synced and the leaderboard fair.
- **Display:** Kahoot-style — question + multiple choice answers.
- **Lock rule:** first answer is locked in; players **cannot** change it (💡 assumption).
- **Resolution:** window closes on timer OR all-answered.
- **Feedback:** show correct answer + who got it, then apply boosts/slowdowns and resume.
- **Question count:** 20 per game (💡 default; configurable per quiz).
- ✅ **DECIDED — question types:** **Multiple choice only** (single correct answer). Simplest to auto-grade and to auto-generate from PDFs.
- 💡 **ASSUMPTION — choices per question:** **4** (Kahoot-style). Adjustable.
- 💡 **ASSUMPTION — timer:** **10 seconds** per question. Window closes early once all players have answered. Adjustable per quiz.
- 💡 **ASSUMPTION — answer lock:** first tap locks in; no changing your answer.

---

## 5. Boost / Slowdown Mechanics

- **Boost (correct):** higher jump arc + faster speed. **Lasts until the next question arrives**, then resets.
- **Slowdown (wrong):** reduced speed / lower jumps. **Also lasts until the next question**, then resets.
- ✅ **DECIDED — duration:** effects persist **only until the next checkpoint**. Each question segment is a fresh "race" whose modifier is set by your last answer. → Every ~question-interval, your climb height in that window depends on whether you got the previous one right.
- ✅ **DECIDED — falling:** there is a **starting floor** at the bottom. A player can fall back down to it, but **falling NEVER ends the run** — no elimination. Falling just costs you height/progress (and therefore leaderboard position).
- 🔧 **DECISION NEEDED:** exact boost/slowdown magnitudes (e.g., +40% jump / -25% jump) — tune during playtesting.

## 6. Multiplayer

- **Modes:** solo **and** N-player.
- **Live leaderboard:** F1-style, updates in real time during play.
- ✅ **DECIDED — sync model:** Each player jumps in their **own world**. Only the **questions and the leaderboard are shared**. Physics stays fully client-side → the server only coordinates *game state* (whose turn to answer, current question, scores, checkpoint pacing). Much simpler & scales well.
- ✅ **DECIDED — max players:** Architect for **full-classroom scale** (30+), but the **demo/first milestone targets 2 players**. Build the systems to scale; validate with 2.
- ✅ **DECIDED — join flow:** **Room code / PIN** *and* **shareable invite link** (both resolve to the same room).
- ✅ **DECIDED — question pacing:** **Shared timer** — checkpoints fire on wall-clock time, identical for every player regardless of their individual height. Between checkpoints, players climb freely in their own world.
